Education Facilities

Education Facilities supports the planning, monitoring, and coordination of school infrastructure, classroom needs, facilities improvement, and safe learning spaces.

Infrastructure

Coordinates facility-related needs and helps schools prepare information for infrastructure planning.

Monitoring

Tracks school facilities conditions, project progress, and priority needs for reporting and action.

Safe Spaces

Supports improvements that help maintain safe, functional, and learner-ready school environments.
