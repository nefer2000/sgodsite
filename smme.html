SGOD Unit

SMME

The School Management Monitoring and Evaluation unit supports schools through data-informed monitoring, evaluation, technical assistance, and reporting that help improve governance and learning delivery.

Monitoring

Tracks school programs, processes, and performance indicators to keep implementation aligned with division goals.

Evaluation

Reviews results and documentation to identify strengths, gaps, and next steps for school improvement.

Technical Assistance

Works with school teams to address operational needs and strengthen management practices.
