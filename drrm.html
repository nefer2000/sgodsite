DRRM

Disaster Risk Reduction and Management promotes preparedness, response coordination, school safety, and resilience-building initiatives that protect learners, personnel, and education continuity.

Preparedness

Supports planning, drills, and readiness measures that help schools respond to emergencies.

School Safety

Coordinates safety initiatives that reduce risk and protect learners, personnel, and facilities.

Resilience

Promotes continuity planning and recovery practices for stronger school communities.
